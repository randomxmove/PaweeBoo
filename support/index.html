Common questions

How many pets and photos can I add?

PaweeBoo 1.0 is free and covers up to 3 pets, with up to 5 photos for each of them. Every care, health, gallery and timeline feature is included at no cost — there are no purchases and no subscription in this version.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password. Open the app and start — nothing is tied to an email address or an Apple Account.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone, and only there. This version stores everything locally and doesn't sync to a server or share it with anyone. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I move to a new iPhone, or back things up?

Use an iPhone backup. With iCloud Backup on, or when you back up to a Mac or PC, PaweeBoo's data is included — restoring that backup onto a new iPhone brings your pets, tasks, health records and photos with it.

Because this version has no cloud sync, that backup is the only copy. If you delete the app without a backup, the data goes with it and we cannot recover it for you, since we never had it in the first place.

My reminders aren't arriving

  • Check that notifications are allowed: iOS Settings → Notifications → PaweeBoo, with Allow Notifications on.
  • Check that the task itself has a reminder turned on and a time set — a task with no schedule has nothing to remind you about.
  • Check whether a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb is silencing them. In Settings → Focus, you can allow PaweeBoo through.
  • Reminders are scheduled on the device, so they arrive whether or not you're online.

Can I use it on Apple Watch, or as a Home Screen widget?

Not in this version. PaweeBoo 1.0 is an iPhone app. A watch app and widgets are being worked on for a later release.

Can I share pet care with my partner or a sitter?

Not in this version. Shared households — several people ticking off the same pets' care, with everything kept in step — are planned for a later release.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual pets, tasks, health records or photos inside the app, and they're gone from your device. Deleting PaweeBoo from your iPhone removes all of it permanently. There is nothing to delete on our side, and no account to close.

I have an idea for the app

Please send it. Small, specific requests — a field you wish existed, a step that takes one tap too many — are the most useful kind, and they do get built.

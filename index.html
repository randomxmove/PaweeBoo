Privacy Policy

PaweeBoo 1.0 for iPhone · Effective 12 August 2026 · Last updated 12 August 2026

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We never learn who you are.
  • Everything you record about your pets stays on your iPhone.
  • The app measures anonymous usage with Google Analytics for Firebase — device and app-level statistics only, never your pets' information. That is the only thing that leaves your device, and it is described in full below.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. Nothing sold or shared with data brokers.

This policy explains what PaweeBoo does with information. It describes version 1.0 of the iPhone app, which is the version currently on the App Store. If a later version changes how data is handled, this page is updated before that version ships.

Who we are

PaweeBoo is an independent iPhone app for tracking everyday pet care. We are the developer of that app and the party responsible for this policy. You can reach us any time at feedback.paweeboo@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter in PaweeBoo is written to a database stored on your device, inside the app's own storage area. Version 1.0 does not upload any of it, does not sync it, and has no server that holds a copy. That includes:

  • Pet profiles — name, breed, gender, birthday, personality tags, medical notes, weight history and photos.
  • Care tasks and routines — what needs doing, how often it repeats, and when each one was completed.
  • Health records — vaccinations and their due dates, medications, allergies and health notes.
  • The photo gallery — every image you add to a pet, including the ones you star.
  • The timeline — the running history of completed care, weight entries and added photos.
  • Your display name — the name you type during onboarding, kept only so the app can show it back to you.

Because this data lives on your device, we have no way to see it, retrieve it, or hand it to anyone else. If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted Finder backups turned on, iOS may include the app's data in your device backup — that backup is controlled by you and governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Camera and photos

PaweeBoo asks for camera access only at the moment you choose to take a new photo for a pet, and uses it for nothing else. When you pick an existing photo instead, iOS presents its own system photo picker: the app receives only the images you select and is never granted access to your photo library as a whole. Photos you add are stored with the rest of your pet's data on the device.

Notifications

Care reminders are scheduled locally by the app on your own device. There is no push server, and no notification is ever triggered from outside your iPhone. You can turn reminders off in iOS Settings at any time.

What is collected: app analytics

PaweeBoo includes Google Analytics for Firebase, which reports anonymous, aggregate statistics about how the app is used. This helps us see which parts of the app people actually reach, and which ones quietly break. It is active from the moment the app launches.

What it does receive

  • A randomly generated app-instance identifier, created on install. It is not your name, your Apple Account, or your device's serial number, and it is reset if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • Device model, iOS version, app version and build.
  • Language and region settings.
  • Approximate, country- or region-level location inferred from your IP address.
  • Automatically collected app events such as first open, session start, app update, and engagement time.

What it does not receive

  • Anything about your pets — no names, profiles, photos, health records, weights or notes.
  • Your display name, your contacts, or any way to identify you personally.
  • Precise or GPS location.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA). PaweeBoo never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt and performs no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Google processes this data on our behalf as our service provider. Their handling of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in the Firebase privacy and security documentation. We do not sell this data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising.

If you are in the EEA or the UK: our legal basis for this processing is legitimate interest — understanding how the app is used well enough to keep improving and repairing it — and the data is limited to what that purpose needs.

No account, no purchases

Version 1.0 has no sign-up, no login, no subscription and no in-app purchase. We therefore hold no email address, no password, no payment method and no billing history for you. Purchases handled by Apple in any future version would be processed by Apple, and we would never see your payment details.

Keeping and deleting data

Your pet data: it stays on your device for as long as you keep the app. Deleting an individual record removes it, and deleting PaweeBoo from your iPhone removes all of it permanently. We cannot restore it for you, because we never had it.

Analytics data: retained by Google for the period configured on our Firebase project and then deleted. It is not linked to any identity we hold, so it cannot be traced back to a particular person. Deleting the app stops any further analytics from being sent.

Children

PaweeBoo is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect information from them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will look into it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

In PaweeBoo's case, most of these rights are already in your hands: the data the app holds about your pets is on your device, editable and deletable by you at any moment, and out of our reach entirely. For the analytics data described above, write to feedback.paweeboo@gmail.com and we will act on your request.

If you are a California resident: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA and CPRA, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their privacy rights.

Who else is involved

Exactly two third parties touch anything, and neither of them for your pets' data:

  • Apple — provides the device, the App Store, and any device backup you have enabled.
  • Google — processes the anonymous analytics described above.

There are no other analytics providers, no advertising networks, and no data brokers.

Changes to this policy

If we change how PaweeBoo handles information, this page is updated and the effective date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described here rather than made silently. This page is the current version at all times.
